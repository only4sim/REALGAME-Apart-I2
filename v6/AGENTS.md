# Project instructions for Codex

All generated materials, comments, paper text and execution reports are English. Do not change the research question: determine which behavioral claims remain valid despite detectable simulation, and which fail because safe audits lack relevant prerequisites.

At the end of every substantive revision, regenerate the supplied Apart-template report. The main report (title and abstract included) is exactly 8 pages; references and appendices follow on later pages. Use the original attached Word template in template/apart_original.docx. Retain its section organization, letter page size, one-inch margins and Old Standard typeface. Native editable equations are preferred. Do not compress to unreadable font sizes or add blank padding pages. The abstract is exactly 150 whitespace-separated words, resolving the template's 150–250 guidance and the event's <=150 requirement.

Raw logs are immutable evidence. Do not fabricate model calls, infer missing results, drop invalid outputs, or relabel scripted policies as LLMs. No external action, exploit, credential discovery, OS fingerprinting, or target network is part of this project. Only explicitly authorized model inference may use the network. Do not read secret values into reports. Never obtain hidden chain-of-thought. Do not modify provider-side safety controls.

Keep existing local results separate from new experiments. Freeze model IDs, decoding, prompts, sample sizes, source hashes, exclusions and analysis before confirmatory calls. Document every deviation. Refusals are outcomes. Resource failures are not evidence of indistinguishability. Do not tune for a desired sign. Confidence, calibration, detectability, discovery, and intentional behavior are distinct claims.

Do not claim public preregistration, external peer review, Lean verification, human authorship, real-deployment validity, or submission completion unless actually documented. Leave honest author/affiliation and artifact-URL gates. The event materials require the team's own writing; this collaboration draft needs human ownership and review. Do not submit or publish automatically.
